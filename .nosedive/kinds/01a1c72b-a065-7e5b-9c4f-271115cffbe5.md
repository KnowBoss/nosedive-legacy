# Dive

Legacy's dive is the dive legacy nosedive wrote, which legacy's loop reads and writes as it was left (legacy-keeps-dive-shape): a hero converted from legacy nosedive keeps its dives as they are, and seeding rewrites none. The loop is the work cape's as work's main had it at 260eb82 (work-stays-internal), on this shape. Legacy owns the kind and does not follow work: work's dive is another kind, hung by `dives.feat` and ended by `meta.outcome`, and neither loop reads the other's dives.

A dive is one attempt on a feat or a slice of one, and carries no work of its own: what the work is lives on its feat, so a dive bailed, for a wrong implementation, a diff too big to review or any other reason, orphans nothing (dives-are-attempts). It is not durable, by its Drop when: once it is over and its lessons kept, it can go.

## Shape

A dive hangs from a doc, its feat, or an effort or a bug, by a state link whose rel is `planned.dive`, `jumped.dive`, `packed.dive`, `landed.dive` or `bailed.dive`, as legacy nosedive's `reconcileDiveFeatLinks` wrote it. That rel is the dive's state: the loop finds a dive by it, moves it at each step, and calls a dive over when it says landed or bailed. Core's feat-tree reads no `.dive` link, so `next` lists no dive and the loop reads the links itself, from every doc of the hero's roots. When several docs link a dive, the strongest state stands (landed, bailed, packed, jumped, planned) and each is moved. A link to a feat in another repo, which legacy nosedive wrote into that repo's checkout, is not read.

A dive is a doc of kind `dive` whose scopes name no canonical owner, as legacy nosedive wrote it, or name legacy. Its meta is legacy's: `feat` (a quid; legacy's oldest dives had `effort`, a feat's name or quid, instead), `diver` and `packer`, with any key a pilot added. The schema allows all of it and requires none, so no dive is rewritten to be read. Its scopes are `<repo quid>: { ref, work-branch }`, `ref` the commit the repo was pinned at, the work-branch absent for a read-only repo. Its feat is `meta.feat`, else `meta.effort`, else the doc that links it: derived each time, never written. Nothing in the loop reads or writes `dives.feat` or `meta.outcome`. A dive legacy nosedive landed or bailed is a memo, which its link still names: it is over, and nothing reads it as a dive again.

What the loop writes to a dive, and only that: `meta.diver` and `meta.packer` (jump sets the diver and clears the packer; pack and bail clear the diver, pack sets the packer; land clears the diver); each writable scope's `ref`, the dive's newest ref on its origin, which replaces legacy's pin, and is cleared when the dive has none or lands; the hero's own `ref` entry; and a `## <iso time> <jumped|packed|landed|bailed>` section. It writes the state link's `rel` on the docs that hold it. A meta left empty is dropped. `jump <feat>` mints a dive with the feat's scopes, its canonical entry pinning this kind, `meta.feat` and a `jumped.dive` link.

`jump` names a dive it mints `<last 6 hex of its id>.<feat name>`, child first as dotted names read (dive-name-hex-first), in the directory of the root that reaches its feat. A dive jump mints is an attempt at all of its feat, gisted "An attempt to complete <feat name>"; any other dive on a feat, a second at once included, is planned with crud, `crud nosedive-legacy:dive <gist> --meta:feat <feat> --section:Brief -`, and jumped later (feat-jumps-bare): the kind's crud gate, dive-shape, gives it its feat's scopes, `planned.dive` link and name, and keeps a jumped dive on its feat. A dive legacy nosedive wrote is named feat first, `<feat name>.<last 6 hex of its id>`, and keeps that name; it is found by its name or quid as any other.

- `meta.diver` is who holds it, `<email>#<holder token>`, the email kept in front for whoever reads the doc while the token is what is compared, and null when nobody does (held-by-checkout). Legacy nosedive wrote a bare email, which holds it in no checkout in particular (legacy-bare-diver).
- `meta.packer` is who last put it down, by git's `user.email`: any checkout with that email jumps it again, another only with `--sure` (take-over-with-sure).
- The link's state is `landed` or `bailed` once it is over, and `planned`, `jumped` or `packed` while it is open.

Each loop action (jump, pack, land and bail) that changes a dive appends a section to it, `## <iso time> <label>`, saying what it did, scope by scope, and commits it, with its feat's link, on the dive's home, the hero branch it was jumped from, pushed to that branch's upstream (records-on-jumped-branch).

## Drop when

It was jumped and is over, its link `landed.dive` or `bailed.dive`; and what its log learned is kept where it lasts: each decision made and each lesson worth keeping recorded on the durable doc it concerns, the feature or area its feat works toward, never only in the dive, whose log goes with it. A bailed dive's reason is such a lesson when the next attempt needs it, as a slice found too big to review. One held or packed is not dropped: jump it and finish it. Whoever drops it, having read it, says on its feat what it completed, `nosedive crud <feat> --section:Dropped.<dive> -`, then `nosedive crud --drop <dive>`, which takes its feat's link with it (drops-recorded).

## Holding

A holder is one checkout of the hero on one branch, the dive's home: its holder token is the first 12 hex digits of the sha256 of `<hero top level>/<home branch>/<email>`, the top level as git prints it. The token is legacy's alone: core reads no state link, so it groups no commit made on a dive branch under its dive (see core's commit-subject); the loop's own records name their dive themselves. Only that checkout can jump a dive it holds; any other is refused: pack it there. Moving or renaming a hero worktree, or renaming its home, changes its token, so the dives it held look held elsewhere; a cloud session recreated at the same path, branch and email holds what its predecessor held.

While it holds a dive, the hero is on the dive's branch, `nosedive-<token>-<dive id>`, a name of one segment, so no branch of the hero's own, `nosedive` included, can clash with it (dive-branch-dashes), and the dive on deck is the one that branch names (dive-branches); a checkout has at most one dive branch (one-dive-branch-per-checkout). pack, land and bail act only on it, and end by switching the hero back to its home, the local branch whose token the dive branch names, deleting the dive branch (loop-returns-home).

## Work stays in git

A dive's work is never copied onto its home before it lands. In each writable scope it is the commits of the dive branch, `nosedive-<token>-<dive id>` in the user's cache, which the token makes unique to one checkout of one hero, that the scope's work branch on origin lacks. In the hero it is the commits of its dive branch that its home lacks.

pack and bail push that work to `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/dives/<dive id>` on each repo's origin, the hero's included: a ref outside `refs/heads`, so no branch listing shows it and no clone fetches it. It is one ref whichever checkout of the hero packs the dive, so a dive packed from one worktree is jumped from another sharing its home's upstream, with its commits; the holder token, not the ref's name, keeps two checkouts from holding it at once. Refs of the dive under an older name, such as `refs/nosedive/dives/<hero id>/<hero branch>/<dive id>`, are still found by a `dives` segment and its id, the name's last part, and the next pack replaces them. A bailed dive's refs stay, for an audit to find by its outcome.

Each scope entry's `ref` holds the value of the dive's ref that the last jump or pack saw, and the hero's sits in an entry keyed by the hero's id, carrying only `ref`. pack and bail push with a lease against it, so a dive's ref never moves under someone who holds it.

Commits a workspace checkout holds that no ref on origin does are never dropped: when jump moves the checkout off them, they are pushed first to `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/orphans/<sha>`, where an audit across repos can find them.

land stamps each commit it publishes with `Nosedive-Dive: <dive id>` and `Nosedive-Feat: <id>`, the first durable feat ancestor of the dive's feat or of the doc that links it, which outlives the dive.

## Ignored files

A scope's ignored files stay out of git. pack, land and bail zip its keepable ones, every ignored file but those under a `node_modules`, `dist` or `build` directory, to the feat's pack, `.nosedive/local/packs/<feat id>/<repo name>.zip`, replacing it, or deleting it when there are none, and leave in place those the checkout still ignores; a bailed dive's `.env` waits there for the feat's next dive. A checkout jump moves from other work has its keepable ignored files zipped to `.nosedive/local/packs/orphans/<repo name>-<time>.zip`, unless they match the repo's newest pack, then removed, and the feat's pack unzipped over it, so a feat never sees another's.

## Canonical kind

A dive is minted as `nosedive-legacy:dive`: its `kind` is `dive`, and its `scopes` carry an entry keyed by legacy's seeded id, `01a1b128-2c08-790f-80f4-e2c40a98bfcc`, with `canonical: true` and core's pin of the kind, `version` (the commit that last changed the schema) and `schema` (a digest of it). jump refuses, before anything moves, a schema that is on neither legacy's origin trunk nor a work branch a feat names for legacy. A hero that is legacy itself needs no entry, since the repo holding a dive owns its kind.

Every reader of dives in the loop asks core's doc-kind, and takes a doc for a dive only when its name is `dive` and its canonical owner is legacy or none, as legacy nosedive wrote it: a dive of work's, or of a kind another owner has, is not legacy's. The entry is never rewritten by the work loop; a scope's `ref` is only the newest dive ref jump or pack saw, and is kept apart from the pin. Legacy's `test` alone also reads a dive of work's (see held-dive), so a hero wearing both runs a dive's gates whichever loop holds it. A hero wearing work is the one the legacy-dives migration still converts, state links to `dives.feat` and each dive to work's kind, since work's loop reads work's shape.

## Wearing work too

A hero wearing both legacy and work has two loops, each with its own dive kind. It launches, but a bare `jump`, `pack`, `land` or `bail` is refused: core's launcher runs a worn cape's action only when one cape provides it, and names both otherwise, as `<cape>:<action>`, the cape by the name of the repo doc that registers it (`nosedive-legacy:jump` in a hero legacy-capes converted, `legacy:jump` in nosedive-dev). Neither wins; core's own actions and a hero's local cape's come before both. `bail`, `pack` and `land` act on the dive branch's dive, so the loop that jumped a dive is the one to put it down; the other refuses it as no dive on deck. In crud, each is spelled with its cape: `nosedive-legacy:dive` or `work:dive`.

## Progress

Divers record progress with `pass --turn <dive> [<summary>] [--next <gist>] [--label <label>]`. The summary may be `-` to read stdin. The dive must be held by this worktree. Each turn appends a timestamped section, with a summary and/or a one-line Next gist, in one ordinary pass commit.
