# nosedive-legacy

## Work loop

Legacy carries the work loop, frozen from the work cape's main at 260eb82 (work-stays-internal), on the dives legacy nosedive wrote (legacy-keeps-dive-shape): a dive hangs from its feat by a state link, `planned.dive`, `jumped.dive`, `packed.dive`, `landed.dive` or `bailed.dive`, which the loop moves at each step, its meta read as it is; nothing in it reads `dives.feat` or `meta.outcome`, and seeding a hero rewrites no dive. It has the [dive](../.nosedive/kinds/01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md) kind, its gates [dive-shape](01a1c72b-a0f0-7508-a398-7c47eef04568.md) and [turn](01a1c72b-a0d2-7947-83de-8f2dd490283a.md), and the actions [jump](01a1c72b-a06b-7cfd-9e5f-710dc60e4a37.md), [pack](01a1c72b-a079-7d52-baaa-1a9bba46a525.md), [land](01a1c72b-a097-7036-ae1a-496002396328.md) and [bail](01a1c72b-a088-715d-a4fe-90ea388bb097.md), each with legacy nosedive's agent guidance where it holds. So a hero wearing legacy alone has the whole loop. Legacy owns the copy and does not follow work, which is private and will diverge; work's `dive-branch-dashes` migration is left out, since a dive branch of the old shape was only ever work's, and 260eb82 has no `merge`.

A hero wearing work too launches, and core's launcher refuses a bare loop action that both capes provide, naming `<cape>:<action>` for each: neither wins (see dive, Wearing work too).
