# Dehydrate repo.workspace

Legacy's `dehydrate-repo.workspace` on core's dehydrate-repo. `<repo>` is a registered repo doc's quid or exact name, as for hydrate-repo.workspace.

Core refuses to remove a checkout holding anything origin lacks. `--force` is legacy's: the worktree is removed regardless, and what it held that origin lacks goes with it.
