# Pack

## Usage

Work's pack as its main had it at 260eb82, on legacy's dive kind and the dives legacy nosedive wrote (see [dive](01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md), Shape). An agent packs only when the pilot asks: to pack, park or pause the dive, hand it off, or switch to another feat. Packed, nothing is on deck, and the dive comes back with `jump <dive>`, from this checkout or any other of the hero, its uncommitted work included.

A read-only scope holding work refuses the pack, below; the recourse is to take that work out of the checkout, or to keep it on a feat that scopes the repo with a work branch, in a dive of its own.

`<dive>`, when given, must be the dive on deck: the one the hero's dive branch names (see dive-on-deck). Refused off a dive branch (loop-returns-home), and when the dive's home is gone: recreate it.

Refused, before anything moves, when a read-only scope has commits origin lacks or uncommitted changes: they have nowhere to go. Before it plans a push, each scope checkout of the dive loses legacy nosedive's render-sentinel pushurl, saying so (see [dive-on-deck](01a1c72b-a0a6-7a64-836f-167c23ffa097.md), Render sentinels).

Each writable scope whose checkout is on the dive's local branch, and the hero, has its uncommitted changes, untracked files included, committed as `pack(<dive name>): work in progress`, with the nosedive co-author trailer (see commit-message); its bookkeeping commit in the hero reads `pack(<dive name>): <summary>`. Its work is pushed to its ref on origin, `refs/nosedive/<token>/<work id>/dive.<dive id>`, under the token of the checkout that holds the dive, which alone writes it, so the push replaces whatever the ref holds (see turn); the refs under the older names are deleted, and a ref under another token is left alone. Before anything moves, each ref under an older name must hold the dive's newest ref as the dive doc recorded it at the last jump or pack (see [dive](01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md)), or older work of the dive, else the pack is refused, naming it; the record is the dive doc's, never the local refs that any fetch into a repo's shared cache moves. In each repo it first pushes a marker, `putdown.<dive id>` under the same token, on its own, before it changes what origin holds there, and deletes it with the record, so a run that was stopped, or whose record the home then refuses, is told from one that never began, from any checkout. It publishes the dive's ref first; only after that succeeds does it delete the older ones. With no work, the dive's own ref is deleted too. Publication or cleanup failures refuse to put the dive down, keep local work recoverable, and can be safely retried after partial updates or deletions.

Each such checkout has its ignored files packed for its feat and is left detached at its base, where its branch meets the work branch on origin, else trunk, its local branch deleted; ignored files the base does not ignore are removed, since they would read as untracked work. Nothing under a configured workspace is packed or removed: the checkout's own, as its repo's config names it, or the hero's when it lies inside the checkout, so a hero checked out as a scope keeps its workspace and every checkout in it. The hero is switched back to the dive's home, the branch it was jumped from (see jump), and its dive branch deleted.

It moves `diver` to `packer`, sets each scope's `ref` to what it pushed, appends a `## <timestamp> packed` section with each scope's work, moves the dive's link to `packed.dive` on the docs that hold it, and commits that, the rest of the dive's meta untouched, on the home, pushed to its upstream, fetching and making it again on the new tip when the upstream moved meanwhile (records-on-jumped-branch). A home that holds commits its upstream lacks has them pushed with it, but only when nosedive committed every one (see jump): before anything is pushed, any other is refused, listed, with "push or drop them first", and when the upstream moved the commits are replayed onto it, nosedive still their committer, or refused with the others if that conflicts.

Turns live on the dive branch and its dive ref, and reach the home only when land, pack or bail puts the dive down: pack publishes them in its record. Uncommitted changes are not backed up before a pack: a turn pushes only what is committed (see turn), and pack holds the rest in a work-in-progress commit.

A turn, `pass --turn`'s commit, is on the dive branch and pushed to the dive's ref, never to the home (see turn); pack publishes it to the home in its record (turns-publish-when, turns-pushed-where). The record is built on the dive branch's copy of the dive doc, which holds every turn section, and its lines say `- turns: <n>` when the dive made any (a gate report land left is not a turn). The home's copy of the dive doc, at its tip, is compared with its copy at the dive's base: unchanged, or a prefix of the dive branch's copy (a dive in flight with turns already on main), is fine; any other is refused before anything is pushed or changed, judged on a fresh fetch taken just before the first push, saying `<doc> on <home> changed since this dive began, by <sha> <subject>, so nothing was put down and every turn is still on <dive branch>: revert that change on <home>, or ask whoever made it to, then run \`nosedive pack\` again`. The turn commits are not replayed onto the home, so jump leaves them out of the packed work it replays (see jump). They ride in the dive's hero ref, where each turn's push already left them, so pack does not push them twice.

A home that moves after the work and the dive refs are pushed but before the record is, so that the refusal above would find the dive part put down, is not reported as nothing changed: the refusal says the pack is only partly done, names what was published (each repo's dive ref, with its sha) and what was not (the record on the home), says every turn is still on the dive branch and the other change is untouched, and names the command to run again. The home's copy of the dive doc still moved, so the rerun refuses the same way until that change is reverted on the home, and the message says so rather than promising the rerun finishes; once it is reverted, the rerun finishes what is left and overwrites nothing of the other edit. A home that moves without touching the dive doc is planned again from a fresh fetch and finishes in the same run.
