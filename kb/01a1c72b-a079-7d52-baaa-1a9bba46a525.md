# Pack

## Usage

Work's pack as its main had it at 260eb82, on legacy's dive kind and the dives legacy nosedive wrote (see [dive](01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md), Shape). An agent packs only when the pilot asks: to pack, park or pause the dive, hand it off, or switch to another feat. Packed, nothing is on deck, and the dive comes back with `jump <dive>`, from this checkout or any other of the hero, its uncommitted work included.

A read-only scope holding work refuses the pack, below; the recourse is to take that work out of the checkout, or to keep it on a feat that scopes the repo with a work branch, in a dive of its own.

`<dive>`, when given, must be the dive on deck: the one the hero's dive branch names (see dive-on-deck). Refused off a dive branch (loop-returns-home), and when the dive's home is gone: recreate it.

Refused, before anything moves, when a read-only scope has commits origin lacks or uncommitted changes: they have nowhere to go.

Each writable scope whose checkout is on the dive's local branch, and the hero, has its uncommitted changes, untracked files included, committed as `pack(<dive name>): work in progress`, with the nosedive co-author trailer (see commit-message); its bookkeeping commit in the hero reads `pack(<dive name>): <summary>`. Its work is pushed to its ref on origin, `refs/nosedive/<hero id>/dives/<dive id>`, deleting any other ref of the dive. Before anything moves, each ref of the dive on origin must hold the dive's newest ref as the dive doc recorded it at the last jump or pack (see [dive](01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md)), or older work of the dive; the dive's own ref may also hold a commit the checkout has, its own earlier publish, so a retry goes on. Any other refuses the pack, naming it. The record is the dive doc's, never local refs, which any fetch into a repo's shared cache moves. It publishes the destination ref first, leased against what it just checked; only after that succeeds does it delete other refs, each under its own lease. With no work, every ref is deleted under lease. Publication or cleanup failures refuse to put the dive down, keep local work recoverable, and can be safely retried after partial updates or deletions. A ref of the dive pushed since then refuses the pack, naming it.

Each such checkout has its ignored files packed for its feat and is left detached at its base, where its branch meets the work branch on origin, else trunk, its local branch deleted; ignored files the base does not ignore are removed, since they would read as untracked work. Nothing under a configured workspace is packed or removed: the checkout's own, as its repo's config names it, or the hero's when it lies inside the checkout, so a hero checked out as a scope keeps its workspace and every checkout in it. The hero is switched back to the dive's home, the branch it was jumped from (see jump), and its dive branch deleted.

It moves `diver` to `packer`, sets each scope's `ref` to what it pushed, appends a `## <timestamp> packed` section with each scope's work, moves the dive's link to `packed.dive` on the docs that hold it, and commits that, the rest of the dive's meta untouched, on the home, pushed to its upstream, fetching and making it again on the new tip when the upstream moved meanwhile (records-on-jumped-branch). A home that holds turns its upstream lacks, kept when a push failed, is pushed with it.
