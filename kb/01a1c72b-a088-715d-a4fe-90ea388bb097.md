# Bail

## Usage

Work's bail as its main had it at 260eb82, on legacy's dive kind and the dives legacy nosedive wrote (see [dive](01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md), Shape). An agent bails only when the pilot says the dive should be abandoned rather than packed or landed. The reason is recorded in full on the dive: say what the next attempt needs to know, such as the slice found too big to review. A bailed dive is over, and its refs stay, so the commits its bailed section names by sha can be checked out again.

`--reason` is required; `<dive>`, when given, must be the dive on deck. Refused off a dive branch, and when the dive's home is gone (loop-returns-home).

It puts the dive down as pack does (see [pack](01a1c72b-a079-7d52-baaa-1a9bba46a525.md)): uncommitted changes committed as work in progress, the work published to the dive's refs using pack's lease-guarded, publish-before-delete procedure, ignored files packed for its feat, each checkout left detached at its base with its local branch deleted, and the hero switched back to the dive's home, its dive branch deleted. It does not require atomic-push support. A publication or cleanup failure leaves local work recoverable and safe to retry before local branches or checkouts are removed. The refs stay: a bailed dive's refs are what an audit finds of it.

Its bookkeeping commit in the hero reads `bail(<dive name>): <reason>`, and the work in progress it commits is pack's. It appends a `## <timestamp> bailed` section, with the reason and, per scope, the commits it held by sha and subject with the ref holding them. It moves the dive's link to `bailed.dive` on the docs that hold it, which is how it ends, clears `diver`, sets each scope's `ref`, and commits that, the rest of the dive's meta untouched, on the home, pushed to its upstream (records-on-jumped-branch).
