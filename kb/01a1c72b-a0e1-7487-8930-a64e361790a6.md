# Record turn

Finds the passing dive through dive-on-deck, refusing unless it is the dive on deck. Validates a nonempty summary or next, and a next gist without line breaks. Appends the UTC ISO timestamp (until core's hero-zone helper lands), optional label, summary and Next gist through the current pass edit. It writes no files or commits directly, but for putting the turn on the home, below. The gate entrypoint reads a stdin summary before calling it. Integration tests exercise it through the real launcher and pass, including one commit per turn.

Once pass has committed, it takes that commit onto the dive's home and pushes it, through `ctx.committed` (see turn, "Reaching the home"): `putTurnOnHome`, beside pushRecord in dive-on-deck's file. It makes the merged tree with `git merge-tree --write-tree`, commits it on the home's tip as the turn commit's message, author and dates, moves the home's ref with `update-ref`, and pushes the home to its upstream; on a moved upstream it fetches and takes the home's unpushed commits onto the new tip, up to three tries. A conflict leaves the home as it was, and a push that fails keeps both commits. Neither throws: each is a `warning:` on stderr and the turn exits 0, since it is recorded on the dive branch. A core with no `ctx.committed` leaves the turn on the dive branch, and says so.
