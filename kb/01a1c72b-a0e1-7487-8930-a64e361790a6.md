# Record turn

Finds the passing dive through dive-on-deck, refusing unless it is the dive on deck. Validates a nonempty summary or next, and a next gist without line breaks. Appends the UTC ISO timestamp (until core's hero-zone helper lands), optional label, summary and Next gist through the current pass edit. It writes no files or commits directly. The gate entrypoint reads a stdin summary before calling it. Integration tests exercise it through the real launcher and pass, including one commit per turn.

It touches no branch but the dive branch and never pushes to the home (turns-publish-when). Once pass has committed, through `ctx.committed`, it has dive-on-deck's back-up push each writable scope's committed work and then the hero's to the dive's ref under the checkout's token (turns-pushed-where, nosedive-ref-scheme), the hero only when every scope's push succeeded, and prints `turn: <sha> committed on the dive branch and pushed to its dive ref in <repos>; the home gets it only when land, pack or bail puts the dive down`, the sha being the hero's commit, or `, not pushed` where nothing was pushed. A push that fails, and each repo holding uncommitted changes, is warned of on stderr; the turn stays committed either way and exits 0. A core with no `ctx.committed` cannot push after the commit, so it prints `turn: committed on the dive branch, not pushed: this core gives gates no commit hook; pack or land pushes it`. Land, pack and bail publish the turn to the home in their record (see turn, "Where a turn lives").
