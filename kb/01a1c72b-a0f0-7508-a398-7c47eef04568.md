# Dive shape

## Usage

The dive kind links this gate `crud.gate` (see core's crud-gates), so a dive made or changed by crud keeps the shape jump gives one it mints: `meta.feat` names its feat (or, with none, the doc that already links it), its scopes are its feat's, and its feat links it `planned.dive`, the state link legacy nosedive wrote (see [dive](../.nosedive/kinds/01a1c72b-a065-7e5b-9c4f-271115cffbe5.md), Shape). A dive can so be minted with crud, briefed in its body, and jumped later by anyone.

- **Before**: any edit of a dive that is held, with a `diver` or its link `jumped.dive`, and not over, is refused: its doc changes only by `pass --turn`, pack, land and bail, whose writes are not crud's, so its copies on its home and its dive branch stay alike (held-dive-doc-edits); the refusal says to record a turn, or to pack it first. A drop of a held dive is refused as below. A packed dive, with no `diver` and its link `packed.dive`, is edited as any other. An edit that changes a dive's name is refused: its feat names it, and its gist says what it is for. A drop of a dive that is not over, its link neither landed nor bailed, with a `diver` or a `packer` or its link jumped or packed, is refused: jump it and finish it, landing or bailing it; one never jumped, or over, goes, once core finds its feat records it (drops-recorded). A change of `meta.feat` is refused once the dive was jumped, having a `diver`, a `packer` or a link that is not `planned.dive`: its branches and refs are its feat's.
- **After**: a dive minted, or a doc made a dive, gets its feat's repo scopes, each with the feat's work branch, the feat's `planned.dive` link (its state kept if a doc links it already), and its name, `<last 6 hex of its id>.<feat name>`, as jump names a dive, replacing any crud gave it but `<feat name>.<last 6 hex of its id>`, the name dives were minted with before they went hex first, which it keeps (dive-name-hex-first); a dive whose `meta.feat` changed has its scopes replaced by the new feat's, its link moved there from the docs that held it, and is renamed after it, hex first (dive-names-derived). Its other scope entries, its kind's canonical one among them, are kept.
